# Workforce monitoring notice (template)

> This is a plain-language template, **not legal advice**. Monitoring rules vary by
> country, state, and employment agreement (e.g. GDPR/works-council requirements in the
> EU, state laws in the US). Have counsel review your notice and obtain consent in the
> form your jurisdiction requires before deploying.

---

**Notice of computer activity monitoring**

To help with security, support, and workload planning, [Company] uses activity
monitoring software (“Pulse”) on company workstations.

**What is recorded**
- The name and title of the application window currently in focus
- The number of keys pressed (counts only — not the actual keystrokes or their content)
- How much time the mouse is active versus idle

**What is *not* recorded**
- The content of what you type (no keylogging of text)
- Passwords, message bodies, file contents, screenshots, camera, or microphone
- Activity on personal devices

**When**
- While you are logged in to a company workstation with the software installed.

**Who can see it**
- Authorized administrators/managers, via a secured dashboard, for the purposes above.

**Retention**
- Data is retained for [X days/months] and then deleted.
